# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Standalone HTML/CSS/JS one-page showcase (`src/works/Moove/index.html`) paired with the Next.js App Router route (`src/app/projects/moove/page.tsx`).

## Users

Architects, interior stylists, collectors, and visitors from the PerPixel portfolio exploring Moove's 2026 inaugural furniture collection, material system, and custom commission dossier.

## Product Purpose

One-page brand website for Moove (2026), a sculptural furniture and domestic object studio defined by soft rose pigment, honed travertine, and monolithic silhouettes.

## Positioning

Moove designs furniture by subtraction — casting pigment into mineral composite, carving solid stone, and issuing each form in numbered annual editions rather than seasonal mass production.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: MOOVE (Edition 01 · 2026).
- Core visual anchor: Pale rose sculptural lounge chair against a rose studio backdrop (`photo-1567538096630-e0c55bd6374c`).
- Palette: Rose Chalk, Pigmented Terracotta/Coral, Raw Travertine Paper, Deep Umber Ink.
- Honest illustrative copy: commission requests produce a copyable studio specification sheet rather than faking backend submission.

## Product Principles

1. Every object is presented with architectural precision: edition number, dimensions, mass, and finish swatch.
2. Material finishes are interactive — visitors can switch pigment and stone states and see the specification update live.
3. Motion follows editorial physics (`cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)`), with full `prefers-reduced-motion` support.
