One curved crease turns flat mineral sheet into a load-bearing saddle.

01 Phase I

Planar mineral sheet

Each shell begins as a flat 18mm mineral-resin slab pigmented in the mass. A single parabolic crease is milled on the underside with a compass radius of 640mm.

κ = 0.00 m⁻¹ · Flat cast

02 Phase II

Curved-crease thermoforming

While the composite cures in its elastic window, folding along the curved score forces the outer wings to rise into a self-bracing saddle without straight facets.

κ = 0.88 m⁻¹ · 42° bend

03 Phase III

Locked monocoque shell

Once cooled and hand-honed to 12 gloss units, the opposing curves lock in tension, supporting 320 kg of static load with zero internal steel framing.

κ = 1.56 m⁻¹ · 104° rake
