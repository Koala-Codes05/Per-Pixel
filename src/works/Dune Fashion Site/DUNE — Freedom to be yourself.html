DUNE ATELIER // SPRING SUMMER 2026

Dune is an haute couture study in desert movement. We craft unlined garments from raw sand-washed silk, hand-loomed flax linen, and botanical dyes extracted from desert clay and iron-rich volcanic earth. Clothing for people who move like weather.

Long light

slow silhouettes

Imagined on the shifting ridges of the Rub' al Khali at 17:45. As thermal winds rise to 35 knots, unlined habotai silk robes inflate into kinetic sculptures with fluid aerodynamic grace.

The Collection

Spring / Summer 2026 runway and editorial concept. Each garment is drawn as hand-tailored organic fiber, finished with hand-rolled hems.

Four Hours
of Sand

The DUNE 2026 concept follows four imagined light hours in the Rub' al Khali. Each colorway and silhouette mirrors the temperature and shadows of that hour.

05:14 Dawn

Pale blue light, unbleached linen fringe, and silence before the heat.

12:00 Noon

Sun-bleached quartz sand, fluid silk gauze that breathes with the wind.

17:45 Dusk

Burning copper ridges, 35-knot wind billowing ochre silk wrap robes.

21:00 Night

Cold desert air, heavy sculptural obsidian wool cape with sheer wings.
