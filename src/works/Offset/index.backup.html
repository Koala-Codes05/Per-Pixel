Bitter, on purpose.

Most soft drinks chase sweet. Sorso is built the way an aperitivo is: bitter up front, so your appetite turns up in time for dinner.

Real roots, not “bitter flavouring”

Gentian root and bitter orange peel, steeped cold for 48 hours and filtered twice.

Zero proof, all ritual

The glass, the ice, the slice. Everything about the hour except the morning after.

5 g of sugar a bottle

Enough to round off the bitter, not enough to bury it.

Serve it like an aperitivo.

  1. 1Fill a wine glass with ice. All the way up.
  2. 2Pour one bottle over the ice, slowly, down the side.
  3. 3Add a wheel of orange. Grapefruit, if it’s Rosa.
  4. 4Put something salty next to it. Olives, crisps, a friend.
