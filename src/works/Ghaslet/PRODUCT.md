# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Vite + vanilla JS, GSAP 3.15 (ScrollTrigger, SplitText, DrawSVG, Draggable, Inertia, Physics2D) and Lenis smooth scroll. Chosen by the user.

## Users

Curious food lovers, spice enthusiasts and chefs. They arrive from Instagram (@ghaslet.in) or word of mouth, want to understand what Ghaslet tastes like, compare the bottles by heat, and order one.

## Product Purpose

One-page brand site for Ghaslet, a hot sauce brand from India (ghaslet.in, prices in ₹). The page's single job: make visitors want a bottle, help them pick their heat, and get it in the bag.

## Positioning

Ghaslet sells heat as a dare, not a menace: "Nothing here says stop." The range is read like a traffic light — one starts here, one hits harder, one just wrecks you. Bottles have faces; labels are characters.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Ghaslet. Logo: hand-drawn jerrycan containing "GHASLET HOT SAUCE".
- Known bottle: The Classic — blue label, bald grinning mascot, "The CLASSIC" lettering.
- Voice: loud, mischievous, punchy, short sentences, a little rude, never menacing.
- Brief-pinned visuals: chilli red, golden yellow, orange, electric blue, ink black. Chunky display type + handwritten details, sticker graphics, doodles, grain, flash photography. No skulls, flames, barbecue tropes, or luxury polish.

## Evidence on Hand

- Instagram: https://www.instagram.com/ghaslet.in/ (traffic-light campaign, flash photography of hands holding The Classic).

## Open Decisions (placeholders until confirmed)

## Product Principles

1. The bottles are the main characters; everything else is supporting cast.
2. Heat is a choice the visitor makes, so the range is always framed as a scale.
3. Be loud with personality, quiet with friction: ordering stays obvious and fast.
