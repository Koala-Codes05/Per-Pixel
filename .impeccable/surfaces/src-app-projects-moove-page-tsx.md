## Direction contract

- THESIS: Moove presents furniture as cast architectural sculpture formed by a single load-bearing curve, refusing generic e-commerce product grids and decorative lifestyle cards.
- OWN-WORLD: Warm travertine paper (`#f4f2ee`), blush pigment (`#f7ded4`), deep coral terracotta (`#f2a18e` / `#ec8f78`), sage stone (`#e5ecdd`), and deep charcoal ink (`#141414`), paired with Archivo Black display geometry, Instrument Serif italic titles, and compass-arc elevation diagrams.
- STORY: Visitors experience Moove's 2026 Collection 01, inspect pigment and stone finishes live, scrub the continuous shell-curvature study, compare numbered editions with orthographic plans, and assemble a copyable studio commission dossier.
- FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed split composition pairing an oversized `MOOVE` typographic lockup and live finish selector (`Rose Resin 04`, `Raw Travertine 02`, `Sienna Clay 07`, `Anodized Pewter 09`) with the sculptural rose lounge chair (`Arca 01`), orthographic compass-crease overlay, and direct `Request dossier` action.
- FORM: Curved-Shell Architectural Monograph (candidate 3 of 7, raised by `paper-folds-pleats-deployable-curved-crease-shell`; seed key `eef8c5eb`).
- FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
